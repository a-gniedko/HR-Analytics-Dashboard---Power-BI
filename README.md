# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

![HR Analytics Dashboard](HR_Analytics%20Dashboard.jpg)

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee demographics, workforce distribution, and employee attrition to support data-driven HR decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition rates and patterns.
- Explore attrition by department, salary, job role, gender, age, and work experience.
- Understand workforce composition and employee distribution.
- Identify trends that can support employee retention strategies.

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

- **Workforce Overview:** Total employees, active employees, attrition count, attrition rate, average age, and average experience.
- **Department Analysis:** Employee distribution and attrition across departments.
- **Salary & Job Role Analysis:** Attrition by salary slab, job role, and satisfaction level.
- **Demographic Analysis:** Employee age groups and gender distribution.
- **Experience Analysis:** Attrition trends by years of work experience.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Data Visualization
- HR Analytics
- Interactive Filters & KPIs

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard provides an overview of workforce composition and highlights differences in employee attrition across departments, salary levels, job roles, demographic groups, and experience levels.
